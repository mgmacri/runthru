# RunThru changelog

## 2.0.0 (unreleased)

### Store release notes (paste into both consoles, under 500 characters)

> RunThru 2.0 is a calmer way to get through what you need to read. One word
> at a time, paced to how words actually read, with your place kept for you.
> Swipe up any time to see the whole sentence. Read PDFs, EPUBs, Instapaper
> articles, Google Drive files, or anything you paste. A one-time RunThru
> Unlock adds reading stats, page ranges and the 3D reading room. Your
> reading stays on your device.

### Changes since the last internal build

- RunThru Unlock: a real one-time purchase through the App Store and Google
  Play (replaces the old placeholder that unlocked for free). Adds Reading
  Wins, page ranges and the 3D room. Restore is in Settings.
- Anchor colours are now free for everyone, with screen reader support.
- Flowing Sentence mode redesigned: read words stay inked, the current word
  fills in time with its pace, and the line fades at the edges.
- Privacy policy is readable in the app (Settings, Privacy Policy).
- The camera permission was removed on both platforms; the 3D room uses
  motion sensors only.
- Google Drive appears only in builds configured to connect to it.
- Fixed: leaving the page-range picker could throw and leak resources.
- Fixed: the Google Drive source row overflowed on narrow phones.
- Removed the unused Free Bookstore code.
- Android builds now get an increasing version code automatically.
