RunThru Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

RunThru is a paced reading app. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches, where that data goes, and what never leaves your device. It is written in plain language because you deserve to understand it without a lawyer.

The short version: your reading content stays on your device. The only service that ever receives information about your reading activity is Instapaper, and only if you connect an Instapaper account yourself.

What stays on your device

Clipboard text

When you paste or share text into RunThru, the app reads it locally, splits it into words, and shows it to you. Clipboard reading sessions are ephemeral: the text is held in memory for the session and is not transmitted anywhere. The app checks the clipboard only when it is opened or resumed, and only to offer you the option to read what you copied — it never sends clipboard contents off the device.

Local files (PDF and EPUB)

Files you open from your device are read locally. RunThru extracts the text and stores that extracted text in the app's private cache on your device so the file opens faster next time. Nothing from your files is uploaded.

Reading positions, settings, and stats

Your bookmarks, reading speed, reading goals, display preferences, and local reading statistics (words read, session lengths) are stored on your device only.

Diagnostic logs

RunThru keeps a small, on-device log of technical events (for example, "a file failed to open") to help diagnose problems. These logs do not contain the text you read, are never transmitted automatically, and are overwritten regularly.

Services you can choose to connect

RunThru works fully without any account. Two optional connections exist, and each one activates only when you sign in to it yourself.

Google Drive (inbound only)

If you connect Google Drive, RunThru asks Google for permission to list and download the documents you pick. The file content travels from Google to your device for import; RunThru does not upload anything to Drive and does not store your documents on any server. Your Google sign-in token is stored in your device's secure storage. Disconnecting Drive in Settings deletes the stored token.

Instapaper (the only outbound connection)

If you connect Instapaper, RunThru:

  • fetches your bookmark list from the Instapaper API and caches it on your device;
  • sends your reading progress for an article back to Instapaper — this happens when you pause, leave an article, or finish it, so your position stays in sync with your Instapaper account;
  • archives an article in your Instapaper account when you finish reading it.

This is the only situation in which information about your reading activity leaves your device, and it goes only to Instapaper, under the account you connected. Sign-in uses OAuth; your Instapaper password is exchanged directly with Instapaper for an access token, which is stored in your device's secure storage. Logging out deletes the stored token.

Purchasing RunThru Unlock

RunThru Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. RunThru never sees your payment details. The store tells the app only whether this account owns RunThru Unlock, and the app remembers that on your device. Restoring a purchase asks the store the same question again.

Shared web links

When you share a web link into RunThru, the app downloads that page over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to extract its readable text on your device. The extracted text is treated like any other local reading content.

What RunThru does not do

  • No analytics services. The app contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No crash-reporting services. There is no crash-analytics SDK; diagnostic logs stay on the device as described above.
  • No ads. RunThru shows no advertising and shares no data with ad networks.
  • No accounts. RunThru itself has no account system and no server that stores your data. Purchases go through your App Store or Google Play account.
  • No sale or sharing of data. RunThru has nothing to sell or share — your data is not collected in the first place.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — used only for the optional connections above (Google Drive, Instapaper, shared web links).
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — used only if you enable reading reminders.
  • Motion sensors — the 3D reading room reads the gyroscope and accelerometer on-device to tilt the view. RunThru does not use the camera.

Children

RunThru does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and the effective date above is revised. Each version of the app contains the policy that applies to it.
